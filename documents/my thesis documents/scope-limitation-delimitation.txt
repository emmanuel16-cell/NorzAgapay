Scope of the Study
The study focuses on the development of NorzAgapay: A web-based and mobile application/app Emergency Response and Crisis Management Coordination System for the Municipality of Norzagaray, Bulacan. The system is intended for the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO), Barangay Administrators, Barangay Tanods or emergency responders, and residents.
The system will provide functions for emergency and disaster incident reporting, incident assessment, responder dispatching, geolocation-based incident mapping, personnel duty-status monitoring, evacuation-center occupancy monitoring, public safety announcements, and escalation of critical incidents from barangays to the MDRRMO. Residents will be able to submit geotagged reports with descriptions and media attachments, monitor report progress, view evacuation-center information, and receive disaster advisories. Barangay officials will assess reports within their jurisdiction, assign available responders, manage evacuation-center information, and escalate incidents when local capacity is insufficient. Barangay Tanods and responders will receive dispatch alerts, navigate to incident locations, update their response status, and submit incident documentation. MDRRMO administrators and dispatchers will monitor municipality-wide incidents, view incident heatmaps and evacuation-center occupancy, approve escalated incidents, and issue localized safety broadcasts.
The study will be conducted within the Municipality of Norzagaray, Bulacan, and will evaluate the developed system based on applicable software-quality criteria and user feedback from its identified users.

Limitations of the Study
The study is limited to the use and evaluation of NorzAgapay by the Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO) of Norzagaray and selected participating barangays in Norzagaray, Bulacan. It does not cover all barangays, offices, agencies, or residents throughout the entire Municipality of Norzagaray.
The system requires an active internet or cellular-data connection for real-time dispatching, GPS tracking, synchronization of reports, and instant notifications. Although the system may store unsent reports locally and allow users to view previously loaded advisories, its real-time functions may be unavailable during poor or interrupted connectivity.
The accuracy of incident locations depends on the GPS capability of the user’s mobile device and the availability of network coverage. The completeness and accuracy of incident reports, responder status updates, evacuation-center occupancy, and public advisories depend on the information entered and verified by authorized users. The system is designed to support coordination and decision-making; it does not replace the professional judgment, authority, or emergency-response procedures of the MDRRMO, and first responders.

Delimitations of the Study
The study is delimited to emergency-response coordination among the MDRRMO, participating Barangay Local Government Units, Barangay Tanods or emergency responders, and residents of Norzagaray, Bulacan. It will not include integration with national disaster-management platforms, such as the National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC) and the Office of Civil Defense Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Information System (OCD-DRRMIS), private emergency-service providers, or other municipalities. Barangay administrative offices will be responsible for verifying and managing responder accounts.
The system will not include resource-inventory management, relief-goods distribution tracking, volunteer management, financial assistance processing, or logistics coordination. Advanced technologies and functions, including automated disaster forecasting, artificial intelligence or computer-vision damage assessment, drone integration, and automated Internet of Things (IoT) sensor data—such as water-level gauges and seismic telemetry—are outside the scope of the study.
